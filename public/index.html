THE PO80 SYSTEM

Designed around stored connectivity.

PO80 is being designed around the idea that useful connectivity resources can be acquired while connected, stored securely on the device, and managed for later supported use.

01 / CONNECT

Connect normally

When Wi-Fi or another supported connection is available, PO80 can prepare and store supported resources.

02 / STORE

Store locally

The system organizes stored resources on the user's own device instead of depending entirely on the cloud.

03 / MANAGE

Control usage

RECARGAR, PAUSAR, PRENDER, APAGAR and daily management are designed to be controlled directly from the app.

04 / USE

Use when needed

Supported stored resources can then be accessed when the device is offline, subject to the final technical implementation.

The important distinction

PO80 is not presented as a magical source of internet without a physical network. The technology must first acquire data or connectivity resources while a legitimate connection exists.

The goal is to make those resources manageable and available for supported offline scenarios, while the deeper network-layer functionality is technically validated.

THE VISION

Connectivity should be managed differently.

PO80 is an independent technology project focused on creating a new approach to how people prepare, store and manage connectivity resources on personal devices.

The project is being developed with a long-term vision: simple controls, strong security, device-centered technology and an interface that anyone can understand.
